# College board: where the edge lives

Walk-forward backtest, FBS-vs-FBS games with lines, 2017-2026 (`python experiments/cfb_segments.py`). Miss is the average absolute error of the raw model and of the closing line. The records bet the raw model's side wherever it is 2+ or 3+ points off the line (spreads) or 3+ off the total, at -110, at the line rounded to the half point. Closing-line records use the pipeline's run; opener records use a run that assumes last week's quarterbacks and a typical wind and leave out short-week games, as the board's opener report does. P4 is the SEC, Big Ten, Big 12, ACC and Notre Dame, plus the Pac-12 through 2023. Days are Eastern. Bowls are listed separately from weeks 11+.
