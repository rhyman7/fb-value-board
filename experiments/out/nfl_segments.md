# NFL board: where the edge lives

Walk-forward backtest split by segment (`python experiments/nfl_segments.py`; the segment definitions and the rule are in the script's docstring). Miss is the average absolute error of the raw model and of the closing line. Records are W-L, win rate and return per bet with one standard error, at the price the data records for each line (-110 where it has none). Opener records use the run that assumes last week's quarterbacks and no injury news and leave out games after a Monday night, as the board's opener report does; "same sides at close" bets those sides at the closing spread instead. Wind unders are outdoor games with a recorded 10-19 mph wind, under at the closing total. Model edges are the market-adjusted spread bets with EV above 0 at the close.

## The rule

Tested 167 segment x bucket cells (every cell with holdout bets, across the five betting buckets: opener 2+, opener 3+, totals 3+ at the close, wind unders, model edges). A lead needs a return above zero in both periods, a holdout return at least two standard errors above zero, and a return above zero against Pinnacle on 2024-2025. With no edge anywhere about 0.6% of cells would pass (1.0 expected by chance here; 3.8 would clear the holdout bar alone). 10 cells clear the holdout bar; 4 pass the whole rule. Segments that hold every bet in a bucket (outdoors and 10-19 mph for the wind unders) are not tests and are left out. The Pinnacle seasons are the holdout's last two, so that check is a regrade of the same games at better lines, not a fresh sample.
